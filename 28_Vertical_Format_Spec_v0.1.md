# Vertical Format Specification v0.1

Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-08-28
Master delivery: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920
Primary viewing: phone-first short video

## Composition rules

- Keep faces, hands, props and dialogue action inside the central safe area.
- Prefer vertical depth: character approaching camera, doors, stairwells, building facades and stacked group entrances.
- Use foreground/background layers instead of very wide horizontal group compositions.
- For group walking, use a diagonal or single-file formation so every person remains readable.
- Leave clean upper and lower space for platform overlays and captions.
- Avoid important action at the extreme left or right edges.
- Keep the final party reveal centered on the doorway, with smoke and light expanding vertically.

## Shot-list changes

- S016 and S018 become depth-based group shots rather than wide side profiles.
- S021 uses a tall apartment facade and vertical sound cue before the door reveal.
- S023 frames the doorway from floor to ceiling so the party feels larger than the apartment.
- S025 uses a centered doorway closing shot and keeps the final audio joke off-screen.

## Delivery strategy

The 9:16 version is the master. A 16:9 crop is optional later and must not control the original framing. Existing landscape references remain useful for identity and environment, but all new keyframes should be generated vertically.

## Generation constraint

Every new image or video request must specify `9:16 vertical` and `1080x1920` target framing where supported. Paid video generation remains approval-gated.
