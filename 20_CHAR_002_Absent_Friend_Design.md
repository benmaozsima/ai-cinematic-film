# CHAR_002 - Absent Friend Design

Status: DESIGNED
Version: v0.1
Project concept: Candidate G - The Visitor in the Green House

## Dramatic function

CHAR_002 is the absent center of the story. He should feel alive in the film before the audience knows what happened to him. His identity is assembled from objects, handwriting, remembered habits and contradictions between witnesses.

## Base design

- Age when last seen: approximately 60-70
- Appearance: warmer and less formal than CHAR_001
- Hair: dark or dark-gray, slightly untidy
- Gesture: expressive hands; a habit of tapping twice on a doorframe or table
- Social presence: direct, funny in a dry way, difficult to impress
- Moral position: not a saint and not a villain; he was hurt by CHAR_001's silence but also made choices that damaged the friendship

## How to show him economically

For the pilot, use only traces:

- a framed photograph seen briefly and never held perfectly front-on
- a blue work jacket hanging behind a door
- handwriting on an envelope
- a cup left in a cafe
- another person imitating his two-tap habit
- one short memory insert with a partial profile or hands, not a full dialogue scene

This keeps the mystery strong and avoids spending generation budget on a second fully consistent face before the story requires it.

## Relationship to CHAR_001

They were once close enough to share a daily route and private jokes. The rupture came when CHAR_002 needed CHAR_001 to speak publicly or take responsibility, and CHAR_001 stayed silent. CHAR_001 has converted guilt into routine: visiting everyone except the one person he actually needs to face.

## Reveal rule

The film should not answer whether CHAR_002 is dead, has left, or is refusing contact until the final act. The emotional truth is certain even while the factual truth remains partially open: CHAR_001 has been using the route to avoid accountability.

## Reference requirements

- Handwriting sample on the envelope
- Prop and wardrobe reference
- One photograph reference, preferably obscured or partial
- Optional younger memory reference only after the screenplay locks the reveal

## Approval gate

Do not generate a full facial reference for CHAR_002 until the screenplay decides whether the audience ever sees him clearly.
