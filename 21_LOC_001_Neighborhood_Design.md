# LOC_001 - The Old Neighborhood

Status: DESIGNED
Version: v0.1
Project concept: Candidate G - The Visitor in the Green House

## Purpose

The neighborhood is the film's external memory. CHAR_001 moves through ordinary places whose meaning changes as his emotional truth becomes visible.

## Fixed geography

1. Apartment entrance where CHAR_001 begins.
2. Narrow sidewalk with one distinctive tree and a low wall.
3. Small cafe frontage with two outdoor tables.
4. Stairwell and courtyard where the second walker joins.
5. Green house with faded green walls and a red door.

The camera may change position, but the physical relationships must not change. The green house is always farther than it first appears.

## Realism rules

- Use believable Israeli apartment architecture without copying a specific private address.
- Keep signs and storefront text minimal or absent to avoid generation errors.
- Use few background people, mostly distant and non-identifiable.
- Avoid dramatic weather in the pilot; light, shadow and sound carry the progression.
- Preserve chipped paint, uneven pavement, old shutters, faded tiles and repaired concrete.

## Camera rules

- Preparation and doorway shots: locked or very slow push-ins.
- Walking shots: measured lateral tracking or rear follow, never frantic handheld.
- Repeated-route shots: repeat one composition with a small change in distance, group size or light.
- Green house: increasingly centered compositions, as if the location is pulling CHAR_001 toward it.

## Lighting progression

Morning: warm cream light with soft shadows.

Middle route: neutral daylight with slightly flattened contrast.

Final approach: cool pale-blue shadows against the remaining warm light inside the house.

## Reference requirements

- Wide establishing view of the neighborhood
- Apartment entrance
- Sidewalk with tree and low wall
- Cafe frontage
- Courtyard and stairwell
- Exterior and entry view of the green house
- Interior hall aligned with the red door

All future prompts must reference `LOC_001` or `LOC_002` and preserve the fixed geography.

## Approval gate

Before paid video generation, approve a still-image location pack containing the required angles and a simple route diagram.
