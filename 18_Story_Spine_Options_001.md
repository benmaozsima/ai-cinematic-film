# Story Spine Options 001

Project concept: Candidate G - The Visitor in the Green House
Status: IDEA

## Option A - The Friend Who Died

The friend died months ago, but CHAR_001 never attended the funeral and continues visiting the old address. Neighbors gradually reveal that the friend left him one final task: return a small object to the green house. The final door opens onto an empty room prepared for the visit.

Tone: grounded grief drama.
Strength: clearest emotional arc and easiest to understand.
Risk: can become predictable unless the final object carries a surprising shared history.

## Option B - The Friend Who Chose to Disappear

The friend deliberately vanished after a betrayal between them. CHAR_001 follows a chain of old addresses because he believes the friend wants to be found. At the green house, he discovers letters showing that every visit was arranged to keep him walking until he was ready to hear the truth.

Tone: realistic mystery with emotional payoff.
Strength: gives the film forward momentum and clear visual clues.
Risk: the mystery must remain simple enough for a short film.

## Option C - The Friend Who Exists Only in the Route

No one else remembers the friend, but the neighborhood still contains physical traces of him: a doorbell, a photograph, a cafe table and a name scratched beneath paint. CHAR_001's route becomes a map of memory. At the final house, he meets a younger person who carries the friend's old habit and realizes the route has been passed on.

Tone: restrained magical realism.
Strength: most distinctive and poetic; supports a memorable final image.
Risk: requires precise storytelling so it feels intentional rather than ambiguous.

## Recommendation

Option B is the strongest first screenplay candidate. It gives us a real adult conflict, a sequence of visits, controlled locations and visual clues, while leaving room for one restrained uncanny detail. Option C is the strongest alternative if the project should lean more poetic and symbolic.

## Decision gate

Select A, B or C before creating CHAR_002, the friend, and before writing the screenplay beat sheet.
