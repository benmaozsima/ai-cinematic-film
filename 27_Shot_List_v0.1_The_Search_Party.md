## Editorial rhythm

- S001-S008: elegant and sincere, with the ritual clearly readable.
- S009-S018: faster accumulation, using match cuts on doors, walking and props.
- S019-S020: one honest pause so the search has emotional value.
- S021-S025: suspense, reveal, punchline and immediate social absorption.

## Continuity anchors

- CHAR_001 wardrobe and face remain fixed across every shot.
- Hat and stick stay with CHAR_001 until the party reveal.
- RINA's oversized bag, MOTI's coffee and AVI's giant key recur in group shots.
- The group grows in a controlled order: YOSSI -> RINA -> MOTI -> AVI -> neighbor.
- The same street direction and building entrance must be used in S008, S011, S016, S018 and S021.
- Smoke and colored party light begin only at S023 so the reveal has maximum contrast.

## Generation plan

- Generate still keyframes first for S001, S005, S006, S009, S014, S016, S021 and S023.
- Use image-to-video for character movement and doorway action.
- Build S016 and S018 from short group clips, not one long crowd generation.

## Audio plan

- Original music only, with a light walking rhythm that gradually becomes party percussion.
- Record dialogue separately for clean timing and lip-sync decisions.
- Use footsteps, cane taps, doorbells, key jingle, cafe ambience and muffled bass as recurring sound anchors.
