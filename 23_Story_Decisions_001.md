# Story Decisions 001

Project: The Visitor in the Green House
Status: IDEA

## 1. What caused the rupture?

**A - Workplace silence:** CHAR_002 was blamed publicly for a failure at a municipal workplace. CHAR_001 knew the truth but stayed silent to protect his job and pension.

**B - Family silence:** CHAR_002 asked CHAR_001 to tell the truth about a family event. CHAR_001 chose peace and allowed the family lie to continue.

**C - Public humiliation:** CHAR_002 was humiliated in front of the neighborhood. CHAR_001 could have defended him but pretended not to see.

Recommendation: **A**. It is adult, concrete, visually grounded and easy to understand without a long backstory.

## 2. How is the final message delivered?

**A - Handwritten note:** cheapest and most intimate; lets us avoid a long talking-head or lip-sync shot.

**B - Old audio recording:** emotionally powerful and sound-led; requires a convincing voice and careful audio design.

**C - Both:** a short handwritten line followed by a brief recording fragment.

Recommendation: **C**, with the note carrying the factual clue and the audio carrying the emotional weight.

## 3. Is CHAR_002 alive?

**A - Alive but unreachable:** realistic and morally direct.

**B - Dead:** clear grief story, but closer to a conventional mourning narrative.

**C - Deliberately unresolved:** preserves mystery, but risks weakening the moral clarity.

Recommendation: **A**. The film is about accountability, not death. CHAR_002 chose distance, and CHAR_001 must respect that choice.

## 4. What does the final doorbell mean?

**A - Ordinary cause:** wind, a loose mechanism or a neighbor; the change belongs entirely to CHAR_001.

**B - CHAR_002 may be nearby:** a restrained mystery hint.

**C - Symbolic repetition:** the doorbell is in CHAR_001's memory, showing the ritual still calls to him.

Recommendation: **A**. A completely realistic ending gives the moral more force. The audience does not need a supernatural explanation.

## Recommended canon draft

CHAR_002 was blamed for a municipal workplace failure. CHAR_001 knew the truth but stayed silent to protect his own security. CHAR_002 left the neighborhood and remains alive but unreachable. At the green house, CHAR_001 finds a handwritten clue and hears a short old recording. The final doorbell has an ordinary cause. CHAR_001 leaves his hat and walking stick behind and walks a new route.

This canon is still a recommendation, not an approved lock, until Ben confirms it.
