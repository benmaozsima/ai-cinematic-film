# CHAR_001 - Protagonist Design

Status: DESIGNED
Version: v0.1
Project concept: Candidate G - The Visitor in the Green House

## Dramatic function

The protagonist is an older Israeli man who repeats a daily visit to a friend who is no longer reachable. The repetition begins as a gentle ritual and gradually becomes evidence that he is refusing to accept a loss.

## Base design

- Age range: 65-75
- Nationality/cultural context: Israeli
- Build: lean-to-average, ordinary rather than heroic
- Face: natural and recognizable; no stylization that changes identity
- Hair: short, neatly combed silver-gray hair
- Movement: careful, economical and slightly slower than the surrounding world
- Presence: polite, private, self-contained
- Acting rule: grief appears first through pauses, attention and routine, not large expressions

## Wardrobe lock for the pilot

- Dark charcoal wool trousers
- Pale cream or light-blue button shirt
- Brown cardigan or simple dark jacket
- Polished dark shoes
- Simple dark hat for exterior walking shots
- Plain walking stick with a dark wooden handle

No logos, visible text, modern athletic materials or rapid wardrobe changes.

## Continuity anchors

1. The polished shoes are seen in preparation inserts and walking shots.
2. The hat appears only outdoors and remains the same shape.
3. The walking stick is held in the same hand unless the shot specifically requires a change.
4. The shirt collar, cardigan/jacket and trouser silhouette remain stable.
5. The face, hairline, hair color, body proportions and apparent age must not drift between shots.
6. The character's walking rhythm is measured: three or four quiet steps, a pause, then forward movement.

## Performance direction

He is not a caricature of an old man and never plays the material as a children's character. He behaves as someone who has performed the same route for years and knows every detail of it. In early shots he is focused on procedure. In later shots he begins noticing details that contradict his memory.

## Reference requirements before generation

- Neutral front portrait
- Three-quarter portrait
- Full-body standing pose
- Full-body walking pose with hat and stick
- Wardrobe reference on a neutral background
- Expression sheet: neutral, polite greeting, uncertainty, grief held back

All future shot prompts should reference `CHAR_001` and use only approved reference images once created.

## Open decisions

- Whether the friend died, left, or is intentionally unreachable
- Whether the protagonist has a family member who appears in the final film
- Exact neighborhood and apartment interior
