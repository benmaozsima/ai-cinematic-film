# Production Bible

This file becomes active in Phase 3. Do not invent original-film content during Phase 1.

## Rule

Approved designs are stable. Once an element is approved, preserve its description, proportions, references, materials, colors, lighting rules, clothing, silhouette, and continuity constraints unless a new decision explicitly revises it.
